Sancocho de Gallina

Hearty traditional chicken soup with root vegetables

90-120 minutes 4-6 people Medium $35

Colombia's Soul in a Bowl

Our Sancocho de Gallina is a loving tribute to Colombian family traditions. This robust soup features free-range hen slowly simmered with yuca, plantain, corn, and potatoes until the broth becomes rich and golden. Each bowl is a complete meal, served with white rice, avocado, and hogao sauce. This is comfort food that has nourished Colombian families for generations.

Premium Ingredients

  • Free-range hen pieces (800g)
  • Fresh yuca and potatoes
  • Green plantain and corn on cob
  • Cilantro, scallions, and garlic
  • Served with rice and avocado

Perfect Pairings

  • Fresh lime wedges - Essential companion
  • Hogao sauce - Traditional topping
  • Cold beer or lemonade - Refreshing balance

Dietary Information

Allergens: None major

Options: Gluten-free, Dairy-free

Calories: 580 kcal

Protein: 42g

Chef's Note

"Sancocho cannot be rushed. We simmer our hen for over two hours to create that deep, flavorful broth that defines this dish. The vegetables are added in stages to ensure each one is perfectly cooked. This is grandmother's cooking, elevated with restaurant-quality ingredients."

Origin & Tradition

Sancocho is the heart of Colombian family gatherings and Sunday meals. Each region has its version, but the coastal sancocho de gallina is known for its clarity and rich flavor. This dish represents Colombian hospitality, abundance, and the importance of sharing meals with loved ones.
