Langostinos al Ajillo

Jumbo prawns sautéed in garlic-infused olive oil

15 minutes 2-3 people Easy $38

Spanish Elegance Meets Caribbean Seafood

Our Langostinos al Ajillo features premium jumbo prawns from Caribbean waters, perfectly sautéed in extra virgin olive oil infused with fresh garlic and a hint of guindilla pepper. This Spanish-inspired dish showcases the natural sweetness of our prawns while the aromatic garlic oil creates an irresistible sauce perfect for dipping our house-made crusty bread.

Premium Ingredients

  • Fresh jumbo Caribbean prawns (400g)
  • Spanish extra virgin olive oil
  • Fresh garlic cloves (8-10)
  • Guindilla pepper for heat
  • Fresh parsley and white wine

Perfect Pairings

  • Albariño - Complements garlic beautifully
  • Verdejo - Spanish white wine classic
  • Crusty bread - Essential for the sauce

Dietary Information

Allergens: Shellfish, Garlic

Options: Gluten-free, Dairy-free

Calories: 420 kcal

Protein: 35g

Chef's Note

"The secret is in the temperature control. We gently warm the garlic in olive oil to release its flavor without burning, then quickly sauté the prawns at high heat for that perfect texture. Served sizzling hot in a traditional clay pot."

Origin & Tradition

Gambas al ajillo is a beloved Spanish tapa that has found a home in Caribbean coastal cuisine. Our version honors the traditional Andalusian preparation while showcasing the exceptional quality of local Caribbean prawns, creating a bridge between Mediterranean and tropical flavors.
