Arroz con Coco Negro

Authentic black coconut rice with coastal tradition

40-45 minutes 4 people Medium $28

A Caribbean Culinary Heritage

Our Arroz con Coco Negro is a celebration of Barranquilla's Afro-Caribbean roots. Made with locally sourced dark coconut cream that gives it its distinctive black color, this traditional dish combines perfectly cooked rice with the rich, nutty flavor of roasted coconut. Each grain is infused with aromatic coastal spices and finished with a touch of panela for authentic sweetness.

Premium Ingredients

  • Artisan black coconut cream (400ml)
  • Long-grain jasmine rice (500g)
  • Traditional panela sugar
  • Aromatic coastal spices
  • Fresh coconut shavings

Perfect Pairings

  • Fried fish - Classic Caribbean combo
  • Grilled chicken - Enhances coconut notes
  • Fresh passion fruit juice - Tropical balance

Dietary Information

Allergens: Coconut

Options: Vegan, Gluten-free

Calories: 380 kcal

Protein: 8g

Chef's Note

"This dish requires patience and tradition. The secret is in slowly toasting the coconut to achieve that perfect dark color and deep flavor. We prepare it the way our grandmothers did in coastal Barranquilla."

Origin & Tradition

Arroz con Coco Negro has deep roots in the Afro-Caribbean communities of Colombia's Caribbean coast. This ancestral recipe represents the fusion of African culinary traditions with local ingredients, creating a dish that embodies the cultural richness of Barranquilla.
